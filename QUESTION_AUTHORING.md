# Question authoring policy

Every production question must:
- Have a stable unique ID.
- Belong to an allowed non-religious category.
- Have a defined difficulty.
- Include Arabic and English question text and four localized options.
- Have exactly one defensible correct answer.
- Include a reputable source reference and review date.
- Avoid disputed, ambiguous, time-sensitive facts unless dated explicitly.
- Be checked for semantic duplicates, not just identical wording.
- Pass human editorial review before publication.

Never place unreviewed/generated sample questions into production.
